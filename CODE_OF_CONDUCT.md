# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

AgentForge is a global, open-source community. We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We expect all participants to act in ways that contribute to a professional, constructive, and inclusive community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful, considerate, and open to different perspectives.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing on technical ideas and evidence rather than personal attacks.
- Acknowledging mistakes, asking questions, and learning from differing experience.
- Considering the effect of words and actions on other community members.
- Acting in the community's best interests.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, intimidation, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and publishing others' private information without permission.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces, including repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and project-related public communication. It also applies when an individual is officially representing AgentForge or its community.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. They may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Reports should be directed privately to the repository owner or a project maintainer through their GitHub profile. Do not report harassment or sensitive matters in a public issue.

Maintainers will make reasonable efforts to review reports promptly and fairly, respect the privacy of those involved, and communicate the outcome where appropriate.
